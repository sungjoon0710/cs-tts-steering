# M2 causal localization

M2 tested donor-informed activation replacement at one prediction position with the frozen Indic-Speak checkpoint, English Amit and a teacher-forced SNAC history. News was the recipient condition and ANGER, happy and sad were donors. Native attention, MLP and full residual swaps were kept distinct. Controls included identity, zero, reverse and realized-norm-matched random perturbations. The final layer-27 residual reproduces donor logits by construction and serves as a positive engineering control.

`configs/research/m2/` contains the frozen protocols, intervention plans, verified gate and text-free cohort index. Use the original private cohort manifests, M1 basis and reference histories to satisfy the gate's hashes. Run `scripts/run_m2.py --help` for the extraction interface and `scripts/verify_m2.py --help` for verification of the owned run tree and immutable source manifests. The verification procedure checks that sampled earlier logits stay unchanged and that recorded patch positions and masks agree with the frozen design. Keep outputs and full tensors in a new run directory. `prepare_m2.py` and `freeze_m2.py` document the original development and confirmation setup. The existing gate remains the authority for this completed study.

Prediction row `j` uses absolute index `len(prompt) - 1 + j`. Row zero processes the speech-start token. Later rows consume code `j - 1` and predict code `j`. Use predicted phase `j % 7` for output-distribution comparisons, including the coarse phase at `j > 0`. The study uses 24 complete frames and fixed positions at frames 4 and 18. Raw continuation IDs remain fixed, and the runner disables KV-cache reuse between replays. Both accepted stopping tokens are tracked. Source bytes for the frozen engine, runner, analysis and configuration are protected by checkout rules.

Development used 30 texts to select the late candidate. Confirmation retained 78 of 80 initial histories and 20 of 20 prespecified second histories. The two short initial histories remain in coverage records. Across both stages, 17,567 interventions ran. In confirmation, native layer-27 MLP swaps had mean own-target recovery 0.7301 and selective excess over matched random perturbations of 0.3852 (95% utterance-bootstrap interval 0.3502–0.4193). Second histories replicated the aggregate selective effect at 0.4324 (0.3650–0.5048), with low agreement in effect size across individual texts. Isolated layer-5 and layer-11 branch effects did not survive Holm correction. Fine phases showed greater fractional recovery, while phase 0 showed a larger absolute effect.

Fitted specific additions had positive relative selectivity but moved away from their own donor in 180 of 234 matched frame-4/phase-0 cases. Both own-target recovery and relative selectivity matter for steering. Native late-MLP patches establish local sufficiency for donor-directed prediction changes under a forced history. This endpoint leaves speech, perceived emotion and donor-free control for later experiments. The ten-outcome confirmation family and its 0.001 denominator floor stay frozen. Separate methods and conclusions for M3 are documented in `docs/M3_READINESS.md`.
