Indic-Speak: conditioning → audio-token predictions → decoder → speech

Observational replay of saved English Amit recordings. A prompt contrast includes instruction and prompt-length effects. Different audio histories are not word-aligned and their differences are not isolated emotion effects. No new speech, training or causal intervention.

Do the changes reach output probabilities?


Full-vocabulary Jensen–Shannon divergence, in nats. Raw model softmax, before temperature, repetition penalty or top-k/top-p sampling. Large JSD is a distribution change, not an emotion score.
