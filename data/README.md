# Data

Contents of `raw/`, `processed/`, and `generated/` are git-ignored. Large data lives on the lab machines:

- Lab corpora: `/proj/speech/corpora/` (code-switching corpora under `code-switching/`, `seame/`, `CSpeech/`)
- Fast local storage on `kedi`: `/local/speech/users/`

| Dir | Contents |
|---|---|
| `raw/` | MaSaC audio and transcripts as obtained |
| `processed/` | Segmented utterances, metadata, train/test splits |
| `generated/` | Synthesized neutral examples and steered model outputs |
